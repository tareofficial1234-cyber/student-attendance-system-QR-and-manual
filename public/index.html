How to use

  1. Add students from the Students page.
  2. Generate a QR code for each student.
  3. Open QR Scanner and select a course to scan students.
  4. Or open Manual Attendance and tick the students who are present.
  5. View or export records from Reports; the method shows QR or Manual.
